# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming, respectful, and free of harassment regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Contributors should demonstrate empathy, respect differing opinions, accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and act in the community's best interests. Harassment, insults, sexualized attention, publishing private information, and other unprofessional behavior are unacceptable.

These standards apply in project spaces and when officially representing the project. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, and contributions that violate them, issue warnings, temporarily suspend participation, or permanently ban repeat or serious offenders.

Report concerns via [GitHub Issues](../../issues). Do not publish private information in a report; request a private contact method from maintainers first when privacy is essential. Reports will be reviewed fairly and reporter privacy respected.

Adapted from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
